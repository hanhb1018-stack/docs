This page covers the **account side**: subscription & quota, invoices & payments, and teams & roles.

Division of labour with neighbouring pages: billing principles and formulas live in [Quota billing](/en/quota-billing), per-entry reconciliation in [Billing reconciliation](/en/billing-reconciliation); this page only covers account, subscription, invoices, and team roles.

## Subscription & quota

- **Subscription plan**: choose a plan that fits your business scale.
- **Quota & usage**: view usage in real time and set threshold alerts.
- **Over-quota policy**: pay-as-you-go or restrict requests.

## Invoices & payments

- **Payment methods**: supports mainstream enterprise payment channels.
- **Invoicing**: fill in invoice details and manage invoice headers.
- **Invoice history**: download and reconcile.

## Teams & roles

- **Member management**: invite / remove members and assign roles.
- **Permission policy**: least privilege, with re-confirmation for sensitive operations.

For enterprise contracts and dedicated onboarding, contact sales or support.
