Complete your GregAPI sign-up, basic configuration, and pre-launch checks in three steps.

## Step 1: Sign up & log in

Sign up for an account directly on the GregAPI platform. After registering, log in to the console to confirm your profile and initial permissions.

## Step 2: Basic configuration

- Regular users: confirm your profile, team permissions, quota, and available features.
- Platform-level settings (channels, callbacks, etc.) are usually managed by an administrator. See [Console guide](/en/console).
- Confirm the models, billing, and log scopes visible to your account. Contact your administrator or support if your permissions are insufficient.

## Step 3: Pre-launch checks

- Verify models, quota, billing rules, and usage scenarios against your provisioned scope.
- Avoid exposing sensitive configuration (such as tokens) in public environments.
- Review logs, billing, and results pages in the console to confirm business and cost expectations.
