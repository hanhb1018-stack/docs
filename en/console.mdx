After logging into the GregAPI console (entry `/panel`), you can **switch theme, language, and interface density** in the top toolbar, and complete most operations via the left menu and main content area.

## Main entries & common operations

- **[Dashboard](https://www.gregapi.com/dashboard/models)**: **view** today's requests, spend, and usage statistics with a **custom time range**.
- **[Usage logs](https://www.gregapi.com/usage-logs/common)**: **filter** call records by time, model, and status, and **export** data for reconciliation and troubleshooting.
- **[Wallet](https://www.gregapi.com/wallet)**: **recharge** or exchange quota, and view the billing summary and balance details.
- **[Model gallery](https://www.gregapi.com/models)**: **browse** available models, pricing, capabilities, and entry points.
- **[Profile](https://www.gregapi.com/profile)**: **manage** personal information and account settings.

## Permissions & teams

- Roles are owner / administrator / member, following **least privilege**.
- **Invite members** by email; key operations are **traceable** in logs.
- **Contact support** for finer-grained permissions.

## Tips

- Menus you cannot see are usually administrator features; **contact your administrator** if your permissions are insufficient.
- If an export fails, **check your filters and time range** first.
- The "density" button in the top-right **switches the interface density**.
