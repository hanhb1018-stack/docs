title: "Console guide"
---

After logging into the GregAPI console (entry `/panel`), you can switch theme, language, and interface density in the top toolbar, and complete most operations via the left menu and main content area.

## Main entries & common operations

- **[Dashboard](https://gregapi.com/dashboard/models)**: view today's requests, spend, and usage statistics with a custom time range.
- **[Usage logs](https://gregapi.com/usage-logs/common)**: filter call records by time, model, and status, and export data for reconciliation and troubleshooting.
- **[Wallet](https://gregapi.com/wallet)**: recharge or exchange quota, and view the billing summary and balance details.
- **[Model gallery](https://gregapi.com/models)**: browse available models, their pricing, capabilities, and entry points to pick the right model.
- **[Profile](https://gregapi.com/profile)**: manage personal information and account settings.

## Permissions & teams

- Roles are owner / administrator / member, following the principle of least privilege.
- Invite members by email; key operations are traceable in logs.
- Contact support for finer-grained permissions.

## Tips

- Menus you cannot see are usually administrator features. Contact your administrator if your permissions are insufficient.
- If an export fails, check your filters and time range first.
- The "density" button in the top-right switches the interface density.
