This page gathers the **support channels**, what to include in a ticket, and quick self-checks for common issues.

## Support channels

- **Console tickets**: **log in to the console to submit tickets** for account, billing, quota, permission, and other platform issues.
- **Contact support**: reach [Contact us](https://www.gregapi.com/) for **human support** and business inquiries.
- **Self-serve docs**: most configuration and billing questions can be located in these docs, e.g. [Authentication](/en/authentication), [Quota billing](/en/quota-billing), and [Billing reconciliation](/en/billing-reconciliation).

## What to include in a ticket

- **Request ID** (`X-Oneapi-Request-Id`): required for call-related issues, to locate the call record (see [Request ID](/en/request-id)).
- **Account & time**: the approximate time range and models involved.
- **Reproduction details**: request parameters (desensitized) and the full error response.

## Common self-checks

1. Auth failure → **check the token type, validity, and permission scope**.
2. Insufficient quota → **check balance and subscription quota** (see [Balance API](/en/balance-api)).
3. Amounts don't reconcile → **reconcile per-entry differences against quota points** (see [Billing reconciliation](/en/billing-reconciliation)).
4. Can't see a menu / feature → **confirm your current role permissions**, or contact your administrator (see [Console guide](/en/console)).

The more specific the issue and the more complete the attached information, the faster it can be resolved.
